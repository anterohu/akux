How it works

  • OBDLink reader: MX+, LX, EX or SX
  • For a Tesla, also an OBD adapter cable for the model. On other makes the reader plugs in directly.

Reader and cables

Battery data is read from the car’s OBD port with an OBDLink reader. On most cars the reader plugs in directly. A Tesla also needs an adapter cable that fits the model.

Reader

OBDLink MX+ or LX (Bluetooth)

Recommended for phones. The phone can stay on its charger for the whole test.

OBDLink EX or SX (USB)

Connects to the phone with a USB-C adapter (OTG). For long tests the adapter needs a charging port (PD).

Not suitable

Ordinary ELM327 readers are not fast enough. OBDLink CX is not supported yet.

The footwell OBD port in Tesla Model 3 and Model Y does not carry battery data. The app’s connection test shows whether battery data is received. The adapter cable is connected according to its maker’s instructions, and the car’s wiring is not cut.

Price

The app is free. Checks are paid with credits that you buy in the app through Google Play. You see the price of a check before you start. If a check stops right at the start, the credits are returned automatically.

Prices are shown in the app before you buy.
