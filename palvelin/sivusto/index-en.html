EV battery health check

Know the battery before you buy.

AKVERI checks the health of an electric car battery with your phone. You get a clear report that a buyer can verify.

How it works

1

Plug in the reader

A small reader goes into the car’s service port and connects to your phone.

2

Run the check

The app guides you step by step.

3

Get the report

The report shows battery health and a QR code the buyer can scan to verify it.

What you need

  • Android phone (Android 10 or newer)
  • OBDLink reader: MX+, LX, EX or SX
  • An electric car, for example:

Price

The app is free. Checks are paid with credits that you buy in the app through Google Play. You see the price of a check before you start. If a check stops right at the start, the credits are returned automatically.

Prices are shown in the app before you buy.
