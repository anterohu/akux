2. The service

AKVERI is an app that reads data on an electric car's traction battery from the car's battery management system and compiles it into a report. On akveri.fi, anyone can check that a report is genuine using its report ID.

3. Credits and payment

  • Tests are paid for with credits bought in the app through Google Play. Payments are processed by Google, and Google Play's terms also apply to purchases.
  • The price of a test in credits depends on the test type and is shown in the app before the test starts. The price covers the test and its report, and the credits are charged when the test starts.
  • If a test is interrupted before the report is ready, the credits are returned automatically when the interruption occurs within the time stated in the app from the start of the test. If a test is interrupted later for a reason attributable to AKVERI, the credits are returned on request.
  • Credits belong to the account and cannot be exchanged for money. A purchase cancelled or refunded on Google Play removes the corresponding credits.

4. Report verification

A report is registered with the service when the test ends. The verification page shows the report ID, the test type, the car's make and model, the beginning and end of the VIN, the odometer reading, who carried out the test and the main results. A report may be invalidated if it is found to be based on a faulty test or if the app has been used in breach of these terms.

5. Nature of the results and limitation of liability

The results in the report are read from the car's battery management system and calculated and verified by AKVERI; the person who carries out the test cannot change them. The report describes the condition of the battery at the time of the test, and its accuracy is stated in the report. The report is not a guarantee of the condition or durability of the battery or the vehicle. The service provider is not responsible for how the test is carried out, for the handling of the car during the test or for decisions based on the results. In all cases, liability is limited to the price paid for the test in question, unless mandatory law requires otherwise.

6. Prohibited use

The app may not be modified, reverse engineered or used in a way that circumvents the charging of credits or the verification of reports. Altering the contents of a report is prohibited.
