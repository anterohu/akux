What data is processed

  • App account: a random account ID and a hash of its secret key. No name, email address or phone number is linked to the account.
  • Purchases: the Google Play order number, the product purchased and the time of purchase. Payment details are processed by Google and are not passed on to AKVERI.
  • Registered reports: the report ID, the test type and time, the car's make and model, the first three and last six characters of the VIN, a hash of the VIN, the odometer reading, the test results and a checksum of the raw data. The registration plate, the car's owner and the name of the person who carried out the test are not stored in the service.
  • Messages: the name, email address and message sent with the contact form.
  • Technical logs: IP addresses are processed temporarily to prevent misuse (rate limiting).

Purpose and legal basis

Data is processed to provide the service (contract), to keep records of credits (legal obligation and contract), to verify that reports are genuine (legitimate interest: the recipient of a report can confirm that it is genuine) and to reply to messages (legitimate interest).

Retention

A registered report is kept so that it can also be verified later when the car is sold, but for no longer than ten years. Records of credits are kept for the period required by the Finnish Accounting Act. Messages are deleted once the matter has been dealt with, at the latest after two years.

Disclosure and transfers

Data is not sold or disclosed for marketing. To verify purchases, the service connects to Google's interface. The server is located in the EU.

Cookies and tracking

The website does not use tracking or advertising cookies or third-party analytics. Only a strictly necessary cookie is used when the administrator signs in.

Your rights and deletion of data

You have the right to access your data, have it corrected, request its deletion or restriction of processing, and object to processing. You can send a request using the contact form or by email. Please include the app account ID (shown on the Credits page in the app) or the report ID. After an account is deleted, credit records and purchase details are kept for the period required by the Accounting Act; they contain only the random account ID. You may lodge a complaint with the Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman (tietosuoja.fi).
